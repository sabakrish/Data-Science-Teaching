# Introduction to Data Science Theory — teaching map
Independent conceptual plan connecting statistical foundations with practical data science.

## Proposed learning outcomes
Explain sampling and uncertainty; distinguish prediction, explanation and causal claims; explain overfitting and generalization; describe linear models, regularization, trees, ensembles and clustering; interpret assumptions; identify ethical and reproducibility concerns.

## Proposed sequence
Study design; probability and sampling; estimation and inference; linear models; prediction and generalization; classification; clustering and PCA; responsible data science and communication.
